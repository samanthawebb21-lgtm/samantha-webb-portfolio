THE PRACTICE 04 STEPS

A claim should be no larger than its evidence.

Good evaluation makes uncertainty usable. It shows the test, preserves the record, and makes the next decision clearer.

01

Frame the question

Define observable behavior and the decision the evidence needs to support.

02

Stress the system

Test edge cases, retries, handoffs, and real workflow constraints.

03

Preserve the record

Keep sources, identities, conditions, and methods clear enough to review.

04

State the limit

Separate observation from inference. Say plainly what remains open.
